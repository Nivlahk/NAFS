A featural script for the IPA

Tessera.

A writing system where every speech sound is one square of overlaid feature-strokes — place, manner, and voicing drawn as marks you can stack. Because the writing is the articulation, you can see why p and b differ by a single stroke — and now hear it too.

The Slate

Type or tap IPA and watch it resolve into Tessera, one square per phoneme. Hear the line in a built-in featural voice, and export your text as SVG or PNG.

Tessera — strokes are features; sounds that pattern together share strokes. The built-in voice is a deliberately crude rule-based formant synth: it exists so a change you see is a change you hear, not to sound human.
