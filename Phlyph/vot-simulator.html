Voice onset timing.

The hatch (lips) always pops open at 0ms. The engine (vocal cords) can fire early, on time, or late.

hatch open (0ms)
engine: -50ms
Early ignition (-50ms)

The engine starts glowing and vibrating while the hatch is still clamped shut. Sound is already trapped inside — when the hatch snaps open, voicing comes out on frame one.
